## Notes on citation use

- Cite Matignon/Brandibur/Cong for the exact commensurate stability condition and nonlinear linearization theorem.
- Cite Tavazoei–Haeri when excluding exact nonconstant periodic solutions in the standard autonomous Caputo class.
- Cite Beylkin–Monzón for efficient exponential-sum constructions, but state the norm and interval precisely.
- Cite Atkinson–Fedorov and López-Fidalgo et al. for model-discrimination design criteria.
- Do not cite fractional-calculus sources for HMC/NUTS, particle MCMC, or microbial datasets.
