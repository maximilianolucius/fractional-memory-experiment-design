# AIMS Mathematics template conversion

The manuscript has been reformatted to follow the current AIMS Mathematics front-matter and page-layout conventions.

## Implemented

- A4 page size.
- 0.75-inch left/right margins and 1-inch top/bottom margins, matching the official AIMS Mathematics Word template.
- Times-family body typography at approximately 10.5 pt (TeX `newtxtext/newtxmath`).
- AIMS Mathematics journal logo/header and journal URL.
- First-page metadata block for volume/issue/page, DOI, Received, Revised, Accepted, and Published (left as publisher placeholders).
- `Research article` article-type label.
- Left-aligned title, author, affiliation, and correspondence block.
- Inline `Abstract:`, `Keywords:`, and `Mathematics Subject Classification:` fields.
- AIMS-like section, caption, footer, and numbered-reference presentation.
- End-matter blocks for Author contributions, Use of AI tools declaration, and Conflict of interest.
- Supplementary material converted to a matching AIMS Mathematics style.

## Submission note

The official AIMS Mathematics author instructions currently allow LaTeX manuscripts to be submitted as PDF. The journal also provides an official Word template; the dimensions and front-matter conventions of that template were used to construct this LaTeX submission layout.

Publisher-managed fields (DOI, received/revised/accepted/published dates, volume/issue/pages) are deliberately left blank/placeheld for editorial production.
