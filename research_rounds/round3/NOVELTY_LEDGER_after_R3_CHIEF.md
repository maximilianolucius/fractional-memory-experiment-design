# Novelty Ledger — After Round 3 (Chief-audited)

## What remains potentially distinctive

The strongest novelty hypothesis is still the **joint object**

\[
\sup_{u\in\mathcal U_{\rm common-safe,m}}
\inf_{L\in\mathcal L_m}
D_{\rm KL}(P_{C_\alpha}^u\|P_L^u),
\]

where increasing latent complexity improves approximation of fractional memory and ecological safety is imposed through a Strong-Allee viability floor.

## What should NOT be sold as standalone novelty

- exponential-sum approximation of fractional kernels;
- generic safe OED / safe active discrimination;
- coercivity or singular-value restrictions on finite input subspaces;
- high-frequency attenuation of a strictly proper channel by itself.

The R3b-neg obstruction is useful because it **forces the correct scope** of the ecological theorem, not because it should be marketed as a major independent theorem.

## What Round 4 must add for a real novelty upgrade

R4 must produce a non-vacuous testing statement in which **the same nested latent hierarchy** appears simultaneously in approximation, common safety, and model discrimination. The contribution becomes materially stronger only when the final bound exposes an interpretable dependence on at least

\[
(\alpha,m,A,T,\sigma)
\]

or a rigorously defined subset thereof.

If the Allee-specific term never becomes active once the actual peak-amplitude constraints of the declared protocols are enforced, the paper should not claim a strong ecological-safety information limit; that interaction must then be demonstrated in the atlas near the relevant crossover regime.
