## Defensible novelty

The defensible novelty is the **combination**, not the generic components:

1. A certified strong-Allee predator-prey backbone is used as the ecological operating and safety geometry.
2. Fractional memory is compared against bounded-complexity finite-state latent alternatives with explicit finite-horizon approximation control.
3. At kernel level, latent complexity, protocol-restricted Strong-Allee common safety, and Gaussian testing error are coupled in one adversarial hierarchy.
4. At ecological-response level, the true fractional prey transfer is treated directly rather than through the false factorization of the fractional resolvent.
5. Its principal-sheet pole pair is realized exactly by a stable two-state integer-order block and the branch remainder is approximated by finite stable modes with outward-rounded L1 error enclosures.
6. Those state-response enclosures are propagated to explicit minimax testing-error lower bounds under the declared physical pulse cap.
7. The benchmark and visual atlas expose the practical safety–informativeness and latent-complexity trade-offs.
