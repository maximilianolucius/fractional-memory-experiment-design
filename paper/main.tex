\AIMSAuthors{Ibrahim Alraddadi$^{1,*}$}

\begin{AIMSAbstract}
A fitted fractional order $\alpha$ does not identify a fractional mechanism: on a finite horizon $[0,T]$, delayed feedback or a few hidden states reproduce similar trajectories. We quantify when controlled experiments can separate these mechanisms in a strong-Allee predator--prey system with coexistence equilibrium $x^*=2/3$, $y^*(A)=2(2-3A)/(9A)$, inherited (model, locked parameters, Jacobian $J(A)$) from our previously submitted companion paper and not claimed as new. Four analytical results support the numerics. The collocated Caputo transfer decays as $O(\omega^{-\alpha})$ at high frequency, a decay that no finite rational latent transfer (integer-order rolloff) and no strictly proper retarded-delay model ($O(\omega^{-1})$) reproduces exactly; yet positive exponential mixtures approximate the kernel in $L^1(0,T)$ to any tolerance, so discriminability depends on the latent budget $m$. On the prey channel itself, interval arithmetic certifies finite-state surrogates with $L^1$ error $\widehat E_m^{\rm state}=0.5335$ at $m=4$ down to $0.0070$ at $m=32$ ($\alpha=0.85$, $A=0.25$, $T=12$); with the pulse budget $\|u\|_2\le0.120$ and noise $\sigma=0.10$, the induced Pinsker bound on the minimax testing error rises from $0.340$ to $0.498$, essentially chance. A combined fractional-delay model, equal to the pure Caputo system at $\tau=0$ and to the DDE at $\alpha=1$, is simulated over $(\alpha,\tau)\in[0.70,1]\times[0,0.5]$: delay moves the predator peak later (by $\approx0.7$ time units at $\tau=0.55$), lowers the minimum Allee margin (to $0.238$ at $\alpha=0.95$), and produces interior responses distant from both limits. In a 1512-cell, 200-replicate four-class benchmark decided by BIC, macro-accuracy is $0.537$ against chance $0.25$; PRBS and multisine discriminate best ($0.584$, $0.583$) but cross the Allee threshold in $100\%$ and $35.7\%$ of stable cells, while the safest input (multiscale, $0\%$ crossings) scores only $0.324$. Safe identification of ecological memory is therefore constrained by an explicit safety--informativeness trade-off.
\end{AIMSAbstract}
