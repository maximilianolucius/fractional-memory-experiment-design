\noindent This supplement contains the complete proofs, derivations, computational-layer
specifications, prospective experimental protocols, and numerical validation material that
support the main paper. Numbered references of the form ``Theorem 6.5'' or ``Equation (42)''
resolve to the main paper; objects numbered ``S$n$'' are local to this supplement. Nothing
in this supplement introduces a claim beyond those stated in the main paper.

\section{Approximation: omitted proofs and explicit parameter choices}
\label{ssec:approx}

This section contains the complete proof of the constructive $L^1$ approximation theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:T9b} of the main paper) and the explicit parameter choices achieving a
prescribed tolerance.

\input{supp_sections/part_sec6.tex}

\section{Design: omitted proofs and practical layers}
\label{ssec:design}

This section contains the complete proofs of the energy-optimal input theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:T11}) and the finite multisine sufficiency theorem (Theorem~\ref{thm:T12}),
followed by the three practical design layers summarized in the main paper: spacing- and
cost-constrained observation design with channel selection, the contrast between
discrimination and parameter-estimation designs, and constrained implementation.

\input{supp_sections/part_sec7.tex}

\section{Safety: omitted proofs, face inequalities, and practical consequences}
\label{ssec:safety}

This section contains the complete proofs of the linear safety envelope
(Lemma~\ref{lem:linear_safety}) and the fractional strict inward-pointing rectangle theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:T17}), the derivation of the four Strong-Allee face inequalities used by
the input-envelope procedure of the main paper, and the practical consequences of operating
near the Allee threshold.

\input{supp_sections/part_sec8.tex}

\section{The Bayesian sequential layer: proofs, estimator, schedule, and robustness axes}
\label{ssec:bayes}

This section completes the Bayesian layer of the main paper: the joint model-plus-parameter
utility, the complete proofs of Theorems~\ref{thm:T18} and~\ref{thm:T19} with their
scope-limiting remarks, the nested Monte Carlo estimator and its variance-reduction
hierarchy, the adaptive schedule with stopping rules, and the eight mandatory robustness
axes. The layer is specified, not executed, in this work.
