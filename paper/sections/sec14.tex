\section{Theorem Index and Proof Locations}
\label{sec:appendices}

Every theorem of this paper is proved exactly once, at the point of its statement, in the regime (finite-dimensional, infinite-dimensional, linearized, or nonlinear) in which it is used. No theorem carries a second proof elsewhere in the manuscript. The proofs rely on the structural separation results of Section~\ref{sec:separation}~\cite{F02,F03,F04,F08,K01,K02,S01,S02}. Two editorial rules from the reconstruction are enforced throughout. First, mathematical theorems are separated from computational algorithms (the nested Monte Carlo estimator of Section~\ref{subsec:bayes:nested}, the constrained optimizations of Section~\ref{sec:constrained_impl}) and from empirical observations (Section~\ref{sec:benchmark}): an algorithm or a benchmark number is never stated as a theorem, and no theorem proof introduces constraints or variables absent from its statement. Second, each statement declares its regime --- finite-dimensional or function-space, linearized or nonlinear --- so that linear-Gaussian certificates are never read as nonlinear claims and conversely.
