\section{Prospective Microcosm Protocol or Calibrated Digital Twin}\label{sec:prospect}
The benchmark validated the theoretical pipeline; this section summarizes experimental protocols grounded in the microcosm literature~\cite{E10,E11,E12} for laboratory implementation. The protocols operationalize the same objects as the pipeline of Figure~\ref{fig:workflow}: the safety gate is the rectangle of Figure~\ref{fig:safety-geometry}, the candidate perturbations are the families of Figure~\ref{fig:waveforms}, and the decision target is the four-class discrimination of Figure~\ref{fig:model-family}.

Five designs are specified in full in the Supplementary Material (Section~S6); their roles are as follows.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Design A (stability verdict).} At $A=0.3$, $\alpha=0.9$ the Caputo model is locally stable while the integer-order model with the same Jacobian is unstable ($\alpha^*(0.3)\approx0.969$); a small safety-capped pulse followed by high-rate measurement of the recovery discriminates the two qualitatively. High information, highest excursion risk: a stress-test design, not the sole benchmark.
\item \emph{Design B (fair stable frequency response).} At $A=\tfrac14$ all rivals are locally stable; direct maximization of the pairwise transfer gap places the informative band near $\omega\tau_0\approx0.49$ (a worked numerical value, recomputed per posterior), probed by a three-component multisine solving the maximin spectral program~\eqref{eq:robust_LP}.
\item \emph{Design C (repeated-pulse memory shape).} Logarithmically spaced pulse trains separate algebraic (fractional) from exponential (latent/delay) recovery envelopes across decades of lag.
\item \emph{Design D (latent-environment disambiguation).} The visibility index $\eta_C(\lambda)=|C(J+\lambda I)^{-1}g|$ turns ``measure environmental covariates'' into a quantitative channel-selection rule: if $\eta_C$ is small, measure an environmental proxy directly or change the output channel.
\item \emph{Design E (safe adaptive protocol).} The sequential loop of Section~\ref{sec:bayesian} under the chance constraint, with negative prey pulses prohibited unless the robust lower-face inequality holds, and a stop rule when no safe design reaches the minimum expected information.
\end{itemize}

The Supplementary Material also specifies the minimum simulation matrix (true model $\times$ $A$ $\times$ $\alpha$ $\times$ observation channel $\times$ input family $\times$ SNR $\times$ horizon $\times$ latent-dimension cap) for prospective validation. The latent-dimension cap must be explicit because discrimination against unrestricted $m$ is ill-posed (Corollary~\ref{cor:unrestricted}, in its linearized fixed-input scope); varying the cap across $\{1,3,6\}$ doubles as the sensitivity analysis of declared-budget results required in Section~\ref{sec:approximation}.
