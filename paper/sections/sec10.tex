\section{Calibrated, Safe Simulation Benchmark}
\label{sec:benchmark}

All numbers below are generated by the released code (\texttt{benchmark/}) and result
files (\texttt{benchmark/results/}); none are entered by hand. The full-factorial
sweep was executed on a 344-core server (300 workers).

\subsection{Rival calibration and safe input budget}
The four mechanisms (ODE, Caputo, retarded-delay DDE, and finite latent-state) share the
\emph{same} coexistence equilibrium, ecological Jacobian backbone, input channel and
observation operator; they differ only in the memory operator. Rivals are calibrated to be
genuine but subtle: the delay is $\tau=0.35$ (linearly stable and safe), the latent coupling
is $0.15$. Inputs are compared at a common \emph{peak-amplitude} budget $\|u\|_\infty\le 0.10$,
the safety-relevant constraint. Two tasks are reported separately, because the integer-order
trace $T(A)=(7A-2)/(8A)$ changes sign at $A=2/7$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Task A (memory-shape), stable regime} $A\in\{0.20,0.25\}$ ($A<2/7$, all rivals
  locally stable): the primary scientific task.
  \item \textbf{Task B (stability verdict)}, $A\in\{0.30,0.40\}$ ($A>2/7$): an easier
  stress-test, reported separately and \emph{not} averaged into the headline.
\end{itemize}
The decision rule is Bayesian Information Criterion model selection; this is a
frequentist/information-criterion benchmark, not an implemented Bayesian evidence computation.

\subsection{Exact and validated components}
The analytic gates hold exactly: $x^\ast=2/3$; $\alpha^\ast(0.30)=0.9690122761517083$ to
twelve digits; prey-only and predator-only channels are both full rank. The Caputo PECE solver
agrees with the exact Mittag--Leffler solution to a maximum absolute error of $2.5\times10^{-4}$
and converges under refinement. The primary \emph{computational} result is the exact
linear-Gaussian discrimination (safe by construction), whose design ranking by mean minimum
pairwise KL divergence is
\[
\text{prbs} > \text{pulse} > \text{multiscale} > \text{multisine} > \text{sinusoid} > \text{chirp}.
\]

\subsection{Nonlinear confirmation (Task A, stable regime)}
Over $200$ replicates per cell (no solver divergence), macro-averaged model-selection accuracy
is $0.537$ (micro $0.491$; chance $0.25$). Per-class performance and the equal-amplitude design
ranking are:

    latent   & 0.24 & 0.85 \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
  \qquad
  \begin{tabular}{lc}

    \hline
  \end{tabular}
  \caption{Task A per-class balanced metrics (4 decision classes) and design ranking.
  ODE has near-perfect recall but low precision: under weak, safe excitation, BIC defaults to
  the simplest mechanism, so subtle rivals are frequently labelled ODE. The single-mode

Accuracy is strongly stratified: by fractional order it falls from $0.70$ at $\alpha=0.70$ to
$0.31$ at $\alpha=0.95$ (near the integer-order limit); by SNR it is $0.76$ (high), $0.44$
(medium), $0.27$ (low); by observation channel it is highest for both species ($0.53$), then
prey-only ($0.52$), then predator-only ($0.43$). Task B (stability verdict) reaches macro
accuracy $0.574$ but only detects stability, not memory shape.

\subsection{Safety--informativeness trade-off}
At the common amplitude budget, safety and informativeness are in tension. Reporting the
numerical Allee margin $\min_t(x(t)-A)$ and the Allee-crossing rate per design:

\begin{table}[ht]
  \centering
  \begin{tabular}{lccc}
    \hline
    Design & Allee-cross rate & $\min_t(x-A)$ & Task-A accuracy \\
    \hline
    multiscale & 0\%   & $+0.29$ & 0.324 \\
    pulse      & 7\%   & $-0.12$ & 0.414 \\
    multisine  & 36\%  & $-0.24$ & 0.583 \\
    chirp      & 93\%  & $-0.30$ & 0.507 \\
    sinusoid   & 100\% & $-0.31$ & 0.534 \\
    prbs       & 100\% & $-0.31$ & 0.584 \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
  \caption{Transient designs (multiscale, pulse) stay inside the certified safe set but
  discriminate weakly; broadband/sustained designs (prbs, multisine, sinusoid) discriminate best
  yet cross the strong-Allee threshold. Safe active discrimination of subtle memory is therefore
  intrinsically hard.}
\end{table}

\subsection{Scope and limitations}
This benchmark establishes (i) exact analytic and solver components, (ii) an exact
linear-Gaussian design ranking, and (iii) a calibrated, safety-instrumented nonlinear
confirmation. It does \emph{not} implement full Bayesian model evidence, expected
information gain, or adaptive design; the decision rule is BIC. The observed
safety--informativeness trade-off motivates, but does not solve, the safety-constrained
optimal-design problem (finding the amplitude-limited excitation that maximizes discrimination
inside the certified safe set), which we leave to future work together with the full Bayesian
sequential layer.
