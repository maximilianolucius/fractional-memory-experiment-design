\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper developed a safety-aware framework for actively discriminating fractional (Caputo), delayed (DDE), and bounded-complexity latent memory mechanisms around a certified strong-Allee predator--prey equilibrium~\cite{F01,F02,F03,F04,P01}. The theoretical contributions are: exact structural non-equivalence of the fractional transfer signature with finite-dimensional latent and retarded single-delay models on a compact band (Theorems~\ref{thm:T4}--\ref{thm:T7}); a constructive finite-horizon exponential approximation with an explicit $L^1$ error bound and the associated linearized fixed-input impossibility barrier (Theorems~\ref{thm:T8}--\ref{thm:T10}); exact linear-Gaussian input, spectral, and observation design (Theorems~\ref{thm:T11}--\ref{thm:T13}~\cite{O01,O02,S03}); safety certificates consisting of an existence theorem for safe informative perturbations and a strict fractional inward-pointing rectangle invariance theorem (Theorems~\ref{thm:T16}--\ref{thm:T17}~\cite{E03,E04,E05}); and the exact Bayesian sequential information identity with its entropy bound (Theorem~\ref{thm:T19}), stated with the corrected expected-KL form~\cite{O05,O06,O07,O09}.

The calibrated, stable-only benchmark of Section~\ref{sec:benchmark} --- rivals calibrated at a common operating point and compared through matched input/observation channels, inputs capped at a common peak-amplitude budget, and an information-criterion (BIC) decision rule --- attains a macro-averaged accuracy of $0.537$ against chance $0.25$ on the memory-shape task, with a sharp stratification by fractional order, SNR, and observation channel. Its principal scientific message is the safety--informativeness trade-off: transient designs have substantially lower Allee-crossing rates but discriminate weakly, while broadband designs discriminate better yet cross the Allee threshold frequently. Safe active discrimination of subtle memory is therefore intrinsically hard, which is exactly what motivates the safety-constrained optimal design problem as future work.

Limitations include the Gaussian-measurement-noise and deterministic-dynamics assumptions, the linearized scope of the finite-horizon barrier, the declared (rather than biologically derived) latent-dimension budget, and the fact that the Bayesian sequential layer is specified but not executed: the benchmark's decision rule is BIC, not Bayesian evidence. Future work will (i) solve the amplitude-capped constrained design problem inside a rigorously verified safe set, (ii) implement the full Bayesian layer --- evidence, expected information gain via Theorem~\ref{thm:T19}, adaptive scheduling, and calibration diagnostics, (iii) extend the memory classes to distributed-order and multi-delay kernels under the same complexity-budget discipline, and (iv) validate the protocols of Section~\ref{sec:prospect} in laboratory microcosms.
