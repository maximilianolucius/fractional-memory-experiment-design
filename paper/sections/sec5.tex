The safe memory-discrimination atlas in Figure~\ref{fig:atlas} displays the protocol-restricted lower bound on testing error across fractional order, Allee parameter, and latent complexity. Cells outside the focal Matignon-stable regime are explicitly excluded from the headline interpretation.
