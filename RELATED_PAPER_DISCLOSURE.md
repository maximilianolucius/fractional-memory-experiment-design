# Suggested related-paper disclosure

The present manuscript uses the same validated strong-Allee predator--prey backbone as our previously submitted work, *Computer-Assisted Stability and Extinction Certificates for a Caputo Predator--Prey System with a Strong Prey Allee Effect*. The earlier paper studies computer-assisted certification of equilibria, the Matignon stability boundary, extinction/boundedness, and discretization-induced stability inversion. The current manuscript addresses a different problem: active experimental discrimination among fractional, delayed, integer-order, and finite-latent memory mechanisms. Its structural separation results, finite-horizon discrimination bounds, fractional-delay simulations, experiment-design analysis, and model-selection benchmarks are new and do not appear in the previous submission. The inherited ecological model and stability facts are explicitly cited and are used only to define the operating and safety regime.
